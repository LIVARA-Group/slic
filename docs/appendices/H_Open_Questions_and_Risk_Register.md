# 1. Purpose
This appendix is intended to be the visible register of unresolved questions and risks: OQ-01…OQ-18 and RK-01…RK-20 are referenced by the engineering documents. The detailed register rows are not included in this repository edition. References to an OQ or RK are therefore pointers to unresolved programme work, not a statement that the item has an owner or resolution.

# 2. Open questions
> **Register gap:** The open-question rows (including owners, options, decision criteria, and status) are not included in this repository edition. Reviewers should not assume a question is answered because a dependent document describes one candidate.

# 3. Risk register
Likelihood and impact are qualitative judgements @R@ pending a named risk owner. Rows RK-01…RK-10 restate the Research Pack risk register @SD@; RK-11…RK-20 are added.
> **Register gap:** The risk rows, owners, mitigations, and review dates are not included in this repository edition. Restore the register and assign owners before using it as an operational risk log.
